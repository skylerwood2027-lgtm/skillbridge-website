## Implementation Integrity verdict: PASS
The site expresses one product-specific system: locked navy/blue palette as tokens in
`global.css`, Manrope/Inter pairing, the bridge/book motif, real founder photos, no stock
imagery, no fabricated metrics. Detector flags that were checked and are **false positives**:
- `overused-font` (Inter) — Inter is a locked brand choice in CLAUDE.md. Not an issue.
- `low-contrast :hover 1.5:1 (#155a8a on #1b3a6b)` — in a real browser every navy button
  hovers to white on `#0d1b2a` (11:1+). Astro scoped styles win; the detector misread them.
- `cramped-padding` (20 hits) — sections pad through their inner `.container`; screenshots
  show normal insets.
- `INPUT 1x1` on Bridge Finder — the visually-hidden file input; its drop zone is the
  target and has a 3px `:focus-within` outline.
- Blank areas in full-page screenshots — scroll-reveal sections (all reveal on scroll, and
  stay visible under reduced motion) and the Beehiiv iframe (renders after load).

## Issues

**P0:** none. **P1:** 2 · **P2:** 5 · **P3:** 3

### [P1] Tertiary text color fails WCAG AA contrast
- Location: `--ink-faint: #6b7d97` in `src/styles/global.css`; used in ResourceRow.astro:219/239
  (0.66rem uppercase labels), index.astro:264, resources.astro:232/247 (incl. search
  placeholder), bridge-finder.astro:633/863/893/903 (privacy line, hints, chat labels).
- Impact: captions and "USE THIS WHEN" labels are hard to read, worst on the light wash
  (3.77:1). These are exactly the small sizes where contrast matters most.
- Standard: WCAG 1.4.3 (4.5:1).
- Fix: change the one token to `#5f6f87` (5.11:1 on white, 4.59:1 on `#EBF4FC`); same hue,
  slightly darker. One-line change, fixes all 10 uses.
- Command: `/impeccable colorize` (or a direct token edit).

### [P1] Resource search box focus state is only a 2.2:1 border
- Location: `src/pages/resources.astro:249` `.lib__input:focus { outline: none; border-color: var(--blue) }`
- Impact: keyboard users can barely see they are in the search field (`#5BB8F5` vs white = 2.18:1).
- Standard: WCAG 1.4.11 non-text contrast (3:1) for focus indicators.
- Fix: match Bridge Finder's pattern: `box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px` ring plus a navy border, or a
  `:focus-visible` outline in `--navy`.
- Command: `/impeccable harden`

### [P2] Beehiiv newsletter loader (~810 KB JS) loads eagerly on every page
- Location: `src/components/NewsletterBand.astro:38-42` (`subscribe-forms.beehiiv.com/v3/loader.js`).
- Impact: the site ships zero first-party JS, yet each page pulls ~810 KB of third-party
  script for a form that sits at the bottom. Slows mobile load and battery for every visitor.
- Fix: inject the loader when the band nears the viewport (IntersectionObserver with
  ~600px rootMargin), keeping a reserved-height card so nothing jumps.
- Command: `/impeccable optimize`

### [P2] Oversized logo and photo images
- `public/brand/wordmark.png` 122 KB, 831px wide, shown at 141px in the header of every page.
- `public/brand/icon.png` 248 KB, loaded on the home page below the fold, eagerly.
- `skyler.webp` (1000px) / `david.webp` (1081px) shown at 160px.
- Impact: ~450 KB of wasted image bytes on the home page; the header wordmark competes for
  first paint on every page.
- Fix: export the wordmark at 2× display width (≈300px) or as SVG; resize icon to ≤256px
  WebP; serve founder photos at 320–400px. Add `loading="lazy"` to below-fold images.
- Command: `/impeccable optimize`

### [P2] Heading skip on /resources (h1 → h3)
- Location: `src/pages/resources.astro` renders `ResourceRow` (`<h3>`, ResourceRow.astro:74)
  directly under the page `<h1>`; the only `<h2>` is the hidden empty-state title.
- Impact: screen-reader heading navigation lands on h3s with no section parent.
- Fix: add a visually-hidden `<h2>` ("All resources") above the list, or make row titles `<h2>`.
- Command: `/impeccable harden`

### [P2] Sub-11px uppercase labels on resource rows
- Location: ResourceRow.astro:167/176/216/236 (0.65–0.69rem = 10.4–11px, 0.09–0.14em tracking).
- Impact: "PDF · 2 PAGES · 228 KB", "PROJECT", "USE THIS WHEN" fall below the 11px floor,
  stacked on the contrast problem above.
- Fix: floor these at `var(--step--2)` (≈11.8px) and trim tracking to ~0.06em.
- Command: `/impeccable typeset`

### [P2] Colored side-stripe callouts (Impeccable absolute ban)
- Location: `TrapCallout.astro:20` (`border-left: 5px solid #d9543b`, used on the toolkit
  page), `bridge-finder.astro:627` (`border-left: 3px solid #b4232a`).
- Impact: the side-stripe card is one of the most recognizable AI-template tells; it cheapens
  an otherwise distinct system.
- Fix: carry the warning through a tinted surface + icon/label, or a full hairline border in
  the warning hue.
- Command: `/impeccable polish`

### [P3] Seven font weights (~190 KB fonts per page)
- `BaseLayout.astro:2-8` imports Manrope 600/700/800 + Inter 400/500/600/700.
- Fix: switch to `@fontsource-variable/inter` + `@fontsource-variable/manrope` (2 files).
- Command: `/impeccable optimize`

### [P3] Hard-coded colors outside tokens
- ~56 hex literals in 14 files; Bridge Finder alone has 19 (error reds, hover tints).
- Fix: add `--danger`, `--danger-ink`, `--hover-tint` tokens and replace literals.
- Command: `/impeccable polish`

### [P3] Small standalone links
- "Read it on the site" (118×22) on /resources; "← All free resources" (134×23) on the
  toolkit page. Menu button and social icons are 42×42 (fine for AA, just under 44).
- Fix: give text links `padding-block` so the hit area reaches 44px.
- Command: `/impeccable adapt`

Not counted: 20 em-dashes on the toolkit page. That text is verified and rendered verbatim
from `toolkit.json` by project rule; change only with Skyler's ok.

## Positive findings

- Skip link, `lang="en"`, one `<main>`, labeled nav/header/footer landmarks on every page.
- Every form field labeled; the Beehiiv iframe has a descriptive `title`; all images have alt.
- Visible keyboard focus on every tab stop except the search box.
- Reduced motion handled properly: reveals stay visible, smooth scroll turns off.

- No console errors on any page.
